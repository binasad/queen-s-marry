Privacy Policy (Merry Queen)

Effective date: 2026-02-24

This Privacy Policy explains how the Merry Queen mobile application (the “App”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, and login credentials (including sign-in via Google, if you choose).
  • Booking details: services selected, appointment date/time, preferences (e.g., stylist/expert), and notes you enter.
  • Messages/requests: any information you submit through support/contact flows.
  • Optional content: photos you choose to upload (for example, profile images or reference images), if the App provides this feature.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, time zone, and identifiers required for push notifications.
  • Usage data: interactions with screens/features (for example, which services you browse and booking flow events).
  • Log data: diagnostic information to help us detect, prevent, and fix errors.

Location information

If the App uses maps or location features, it may process:

  • Approximate or precise location (depending on your permissions and device settings) to show salon locations, directions, or map functionality.

You can control location permissions in your device settings.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the App (account creation, login, bookings, reminders).
  • Process appointments and manage your booking history and status.
  • Send notifications (e.g., booking confirmations, reminders, updates) if you opt in.
  • Improve and maintain the App (debugging, performance monitoring, feature improvements).
  • Security and fraud prevention (protect accounts and detect abuse).
  • Legal compliance where required.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following cases:

Service providers (processors)

We use third-party services to operate core functionality. Depending on your usage, the App may share necessary information with:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication, databases/storage, and push notifications.
  • Google Maps to display maps and location-related features.
  • Stripe to process payments (if you use “pay now” features).

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Business operations

We may share information with salon staff/admins as needed to: manage appointments, provide services you requested, handle support requests.

Legal reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the salon, or others.

Payments

If you pay through the App:

  • Payments are processed by Stripe.
  • We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Stripe may collect and process payment information directly. Please review Stripe’s privacy policy for details.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to: provide the App and maintain your account, keep booking history, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

You may request deletion (see “Your choices” below).

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data (subject to legal/operational requirements)
  • Opt out of notifications (device settings) and location access (device settings)

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version in the repository and/or within the App as appropriate. Your continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions or requests about this Privacy Policy, contact:

  • Email: privacy@yourdomain.com (replace with your real email)
  • Company/Salon: Aztrosys / Merry Queen (update as needed)
